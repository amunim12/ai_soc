# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Security Operations Centre analysts and administrators in enterprise or defence environments. They work in darkened operations rooms or at dedicated workstations, managing live security incidents under time pressure. Primary task on any screen: triage an alert, make a decision, move on. Secondary task: review what the pipeline did and audit its output.

## Product Purpose

AI SOC is an on-premises, air-gapped security operations platform that automates alert triage, generates incident-response playbooks via local LLM inference, and routes high-risk decisions to human analysts before executing containment actions via SOAR. It exists to reduce analyst alert fatigue without removing human accountability from irreversible actions.

## Brand Personality

Precise, accountable, operator-grade. Not a product that shows off; a tool that works.

## Anti-references

- CrowdStrike Falcon UI (neon purple glassmorphism, glowing cards, cyberpunk aesthetic)
- Generic dark SaaS dashboards with blue/purple gradients and backdrop-filter blur everywhere
- "Hacker aesthetic" terminals with animated grid backgrounds and neon glow effects
- Hero-metric dashboards with big numbers, gradient accent lines, and identical card grids

## Design Principles

1. **Function before decoration.** Every visual element must earn its place by carrying information. Decorative borders, glows, and gradients are eliminated unless they encode meaning.
2. **Status through structure, not color alone.** Severity is communicated by badge, tinted border, and background — not by a decorative side stripe.
3. **One accent.** A single amber color is used for active/interactive states. Status colors (red, orange, blue, green) are functional-only and never repurposed as decoration.
4. **Dense but readable.** Information density is a feature for analysts who need to scan many alerts. But font hierarchy must be clear — not everything is 10px uppercase tracked.
5. **Operator trust.** The UI should feel like certified equipment: consistent, predictable, no surprises. Animations are purposeful, not theatrical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All body text at ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. Status colors never used as the sole differentiator — shape/text label always accompanies color coding. Reduced motion respected.
